== Quality Requirements

=== Quality Tree

[options="header",cols="1,2,3,3,1"]
|===
|ID |Quality Attribute |Scenario |Fit Criteria |Priority

|P-1 |Performance (Query Latency) |Dashboard report generation |Reports and charts delivered within 1.5 seconds for any parameter combination over ≤3 months |High
|P-2 |Performance (Write Throughput) |Peak event ingestion |System sustains 5M write RPS during traffic spikes without data loss |High
|S-1 |Scalability (Horizontal) |Ingestion capacity growth |System handles 100 billion events/day baseline, scaling to 146 billion by Year 5 |High
|S-2 |Scalability (Storage) |Long-term data retention |System stores cumulative 99 TB of event data by Year 5 with forever retention |High
|A-1 |Availability (Data Freshness) |Event processing latency |Events appear in statistics within 50-100 minutes of occurrence |Medium
|A-2 |Availability (System Uptime) |Service availability |System maintains 99.9% uptime during business hours |High
|D-1 |Durability (Data Retention) |Permanent event storage |Zero data loss for ingested events with forever retention policy |High
|M-1 |Maintainability (Monitoring) |System observability |Detection of bottlenecks and anomalies within 2 minutes across distributed pipeline |Medium
|U-1 |Usability (SDK Integration) |Client integration ease |Website owners integrate tracking SDK in <30 minutes with zero server-side changes |Medium
|SE-1 |Security (Data Privacy) |GDPR/CCPA compliance |System supports consent management, data deletion requests within 30 days, and audit logging |High
|===
