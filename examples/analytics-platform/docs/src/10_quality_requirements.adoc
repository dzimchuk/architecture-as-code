==== Recommended Actions

1. **Advance ADR-0004 through ADR-0014 from Proposed to Accepted** — all 7 High-priority QAs currently have zero Accepted coverage; formal review and acceptance is the highest-priority action.
2. **Prioritize acceptance of Iteration 1–2 ADRs first** (ADR-0004, ADR-0005, ADR-0006, ADR-0007) — these cover the foundational ingestion and storage architecture addressing P-2, S-1, S-2, and D-1.
3. **Create ADR for encryption at rest and in transit** — SE-1 covers consent, deletion, and audit but no ADR addresses data encryption (noted as follow-up in ADR-0013 consequences). **TODO:** New ADR required.
4. **Create ADR for multi-region disaster recovery** — A-2 (99.9% uptime) currently relies on single-region multi-AZ (ADR-0011); full-region failure is explicitly out of scope. Evaluate whether business requirements necessitate cross-region DR. **TODO:** New ADR required if multi-region is needed.
5. **Create ADR for tenant isolation and noisy-neighbor prevention** — noted as technical debt in §11; no dedicated ADR exists. ADR-0009 includes per-tenant rate limits but a comprehensive tenant isolation strategy is missing. **TODO:** New ADR or extend ADR-0009.
6. **Formalize ADR-0002 and ADR-0003 status** — these are foundational estimation ADRs; consider accepting them as baseline assumptions or superseding them if estimates have been revised.
